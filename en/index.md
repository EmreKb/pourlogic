# Pour Logic — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

This policy explains what data the game **Pour Logic** ("the game") processes on iOS, why, and what choices you have.

## 1. Who we are
The game is developed and published by **Yunus Emre Karabulut**, an individual developer, Serdivan, Sakarya ("we", "us"). He is the data controller.
The game is not offered in the European Union or the European Economic Area.
Contact: **destek@emrekb.com**

## 2. The short version
- No account, no sign-in, no name, email or phone number.
- No in-app purchases.
- We do **not** use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites. The game never shows Apple's tracking (ATT) prompt.
- Gameplay statistics are sent **only if you allow them**.
- Ads are **non-personalised** (not based on your interests or profile).
- We do not sell your data.

## 3. What we process and why

| Data | Source / provider | Purpose | Legal basis (UK GDPR / KVKK) | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Game progress and settings (levels, sound, consent choices) | Stored on your device only | Run the game, remember your choices | Contract / necessary for the service (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)) | Always; never leaves your device |
| Random game ID, device and OS info, gameplay events (levels started/completed/failed, time, attempts, hints used, level quits), crash and error reports | GameAnalytics | Understand which levels are too hard, fix bugs, improve the game | **Your consent** (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK explicit consent, Art. 5(1)) | **Only after you tap "Allow"** |
| Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), device and OS info, IP address (used for approximate location, e.g. country), ad interactions, ad diagnostics | Google AdMob | Show non-personalised ads (for example the optional ad that gives you a hint), limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, measure ad delivery | Consent where required (Google's consent form in the UK and Switzerland); see section 4 | When an ad is requested |
| Your consent choice (consent string) | Google User Messaging Platform, stored on your device | Remember and prove your ad and privacy choices | Legal obligation / consent records | When the consent form is shown |

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. Analytics events contain no free text, name or email.

## 4. Advertising
- Rewarded ads are optional. The game is fully playable without watching them.
- We request **non-personalised ads only**. Google may still use limited data (IDFV, IP address, device info) to deliver the ad, cap frequency, report results and prevent fraud.
- In the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested. If you do not consent, only limited ads may be shown.
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 5. Analytics (GameAnalytics)
- On first launch we ask whether you want to share gameplay statistics. Both buttons are equal; **"Not now" sends nothing**.
- In the UK and Switzerland this choice is part of Google's consent form. Everywhere else (including Türkiye) the game shows its own "Allow / Not now" screen.
- Before you choose, nothing is sent and nothing is saved for analytics. If you choose "Not now" or close the game, waiting events are deleted.
- GameAnalytics' privacy policy: https://www.gameanalytics.com/privacy-policy (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark)

## 6. How to change or withdraw consent
- **In the game:** Settings › **Privacy options**. You can allow or withdraw at any time, as easily as you gave consent. After withdrawal no further analytics are sent and the analytics SDK does not restart.
- **On iOS:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking (the game does not request tracking, so it should not appear there).
- Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it.

## 7. International transfers
Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the **United States** and the **European Union**. Google and GameAnalytics state that they rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum and, where applicable, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. For users in Türkiye, see the KVKK notice: https://emrekb.github.io/pourlogic/tr/kvkk/.

## 8. How long data is kept
- Data on your device: until you delete the game.
- GameAnalytics: as long as needed to provide the analytics service, then deleted or anonymised (GameAnalytics privacy notice v2.3, 18 Mar 2026).
- Google AdMob: according to Google's retention policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

## 9. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example the UK GDPR in the UK, the Swiss FADP in Switzerland, KVKK Art. 11 in Türkiye) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Board, Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu; in the UK: the Information Commissioner's Office; in Switzerland: the FDPIC).
Write to **destek@emrekb.com**. We reply within 30 days.

## 10. Children
The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It is not in the App Store Kids category. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes
We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when the policy changes, and ask for consent again if a change needs it.
