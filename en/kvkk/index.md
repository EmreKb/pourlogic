# Pour Logic — KVKK Notice

*Convenience translation; the [Turkish text](../../tr/kvkk/) prevails.*

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## 1. Data controller
Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), the data controller is the individual developer **Yunus Emre Karabulut**, Serdivan, Sakarya, email **destek@emrekb.com**.

## 2. Personal data processed
- **Device / transaction security:** random game ID, Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), device model, OS version, IP address.
- **Usage (gameplay):** levels started, completed and failed, time, attempts, hints used, level quits, crash and error reports.
- **Advertising:** ad requests and interactions, ad diagnostics.
- **Preferences:** your consent choices (stored on your device).

We do not process your name, email, phone number, precise location or special-category data, and we do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

## 3. Purposes and legal bases
| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Run the game and remember progress and settings (on device only) | Performance of a contract (Art. 5(2)(c)) |
| Improve the game and fix bugs (GameAnalytics) | **Explicit consent** (Art. 5(1)), only if you choose "Allow" |
| Serve non-personalised ads, cap frequency, prevent fraud (Google AdMob) | **Explicit consent** (Art. 5(1)), given through the in-game consent screen; ads are served only after you choose |
| Record and prove your consent choices | Legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç)) |

## 4. Collection method
Collected automatically and electronically through SDKs in the game. Analytics data is collected only after your explicit consent.

## 5. Recipients and transfers abroad
GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark) and GameAnalytics Limited (UK) (analytics provider; Denmark/EU, UK and countries of its sub-processors, including outside the EU/UK) and Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (AdMob ads and UMP consent; US, EU and other Google data centres). Because their servers are outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad under KVKK Art. 9 on the basis of **your explicit consent**. We do not sell your data.

## 6. Retention
On-device data: until you delete the game. GameAnalytics: as long as needed for the service. Google: per Google's retention policy. Then deleted or anonymised.

## 7. Withdrawing consent
Settings › **Privacy options** in the game, at any time. After withdrawal no further analytics are sent; earlier processing remains lawful.

## 8. Your rights (Art. 11)
To learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it is transferred to; request correction, deletion or destruction and notification of these to third parties; object to an adverse result from solely automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing.

## 9. How to apply
In Turkish, per the Communiqué on Application Procedures, by email to **destek@emrekb.com** (primary channel). We respond free of charge within 30 days. You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board if unsatisfied.
